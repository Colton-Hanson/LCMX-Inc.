KNOWN GAPS (not frontend faults; do not call these failures)
1. Backend/database: the real seed script never creates balance rows, so preloaded starter expenses are not counted in balances. A fixed copy is only in the test copy of the stack.
2. The expense list has no created-by field and no receipt-link flag, and edit and delete are admin only, so the frontend cannot hide Edit and Delete ahead of time for receipt-linked expenses; the server's 409 message shows instead.
3. Receipts stay at "uploaded" until something reads them. A stand-in reader (outside the repo) was used for the tests; it is not part of the backend yet.
4. A receipt picture cannot be attached to a hand-entered expense in the app (no ticket asks for it).
5. One account's group page title can show "Group" because the group name still comes from a browser-saved list (older workaround, not in these tickets).
